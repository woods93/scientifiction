THE EIGHT-CIRCUIT MODEL OF CONSCIOUSNESS
Reader's notes for The Scientifiction Institute and for JoJo

About these notes: written by Claude in its own words. The Wilson numbering and descriptions come from the owner's copy of Prometheus Rising. The account of Leary's own versions is from general knowledge and should be checked against Leary's books.

WHAT IT IS
A model, first set out by Timothy Leary in the 1970s and then popularized by Robert Anton Wilson, that describes consciousness as eight "circuits" of the nervous system, each with its own imprint, its own kind of experience, and its own stage of evolution. The Church of the Whole Record's talk of "fifth-level consciousness" points here.

THE EIGHT, IN WILSON'S ORDER (Prometheus Rising)
1. Bio-survival (oral): safe and nourishing, or not.
2. Emotional-territorial (anal): dominance and status.
3. Semantic, or time-binding: language, symbols, tools.
4. Socio-sexual: roles and social mores.
5. Holistic neurosomatic: body-mind bliss; tantra; feeling high.
6. Collective neurogenetic: DNA and the archetypes; numinous experience.
7. Metaprogramming: reprogramming the other circuits and choosing between reality-tunnels.
8. Non-local quantum: near-death, out-of-body and trans-time experience.

THE LEARY CONNECTION
Leary's own accounts differ in terminology and in the order of the two middle "future" circuits. Leary's books on the model include Exo-Psychology (1977) and Info-Psychology. When you meet "circuit 6" or "circuit 7," check which author's numbering is meant.

WHAT "FIFTH-LEVEL" MEANS
In Wilson's numbering, the fifth circuit is the neurosomatic one. It is the first of the "future" circuits, and the first one imprinted by ecstasy and not by survival or social pressure. If the Church uses the phrase differently, say so in the lore, since visitors who know Wilson will assume this meaning.

THE TERRESTRIAL AND THE EXTRA-TERRESTRIAL
Circuits 1 to 4 are said to serve survival on earth. Circuits 5 to 8 are said to be a preview of future human evolution and, in Wilson's playful telling, of life beyond the planet.

HOW IT CONNECTS
- Prometheus Rising and Quantum Psychology develop the model.
- Gurdjieff's self-observation and the Exegesis's talk of a second self both describe the experience of the "higher" circuits from other angles.
- Crowley and Leary: Wilson links the model to magical practice, treating ritual as a way to reprogram circuits.

CAUTIONS
It is a map made of metaphors, mixed from physiology, psychology and mysticism. It is a useful way of talking, not a validated theory.
